/*
Q: Won't this break my existing code?
A: No! All existing color names are the same, just in TravelloTheme instead
   of ThemePalette. Do a Find & Replace: ThemePalette → TravelloTheme

Q: Do I have to use gradients everywhere?
A: No! Use them where they add value. Plain colors still work fine.

Q: Will gradients hurt performance?
A: No! Gradients are hardware-accelerated. Might even be faster than images.

Q: How do I migrate gradually?
A: Update imports first, then gradually add gradients to screens. No rush!

Q: Can I create my own gradients?
A: Yes! Add them to TravelloTheme class. Structure is simple:
   static LinearGradient myGradient = const LinearGradient(
     colors: [Color(0xFF...),Color(0xFF...)],
   );

Q: What about dark mode?
A: Gradients work beautifully in both light and dark modes.

Q: Can I adjust gradient directions?
A: Yes! LinearGradient has begin/end/colors parameters - fully customizable.
*/
